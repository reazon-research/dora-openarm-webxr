HUD preview — no robot, no headset

The rear-view window is drawn from the real REAR_VIEW placement in panorama.js, in the same viewer-space meters as the panels, so its alignment with the robot panel is the real one. Panels are placed from their real PANELS geometry, projected into an assumed 100°×90° field of view, so this shows where they sit relative to the reticle and to the wrist cameras (dashed, from the defaults in wrist.js). Angular sizes are faithful; whether text is readable also depends on headset resolution, so treat this as a layout check rather than a legibility verdict.
