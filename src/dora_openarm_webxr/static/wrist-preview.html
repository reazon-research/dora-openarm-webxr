Wrist panel preview — press a stick to enlarge that side

enlarged: neither

The two panels are the wrist cameras as the headset draws them, at the configured width and left_center / right_center. Press a button to send that side's thumbstick down: the panel toggles, so it stays enlarged when the button comes back up and goes back on the next press. Holding a button down must not flip it repeatedly, which is what the harness checks by feeding the button state every frame the way ar.js does. The slider is the wrist_panels.zoom value from the view configuration, read once per frame, so it can be judged live before being written into the YAML. The gripper strip under each video is drawn here to show that it grows with the panel. Colors and labels come from two synthetic cameras, not a robot.
